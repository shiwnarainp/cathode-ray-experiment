The discovery

What Thomson Found

"J. J. Thomson demonstrated that cathode rays consist of negatively charged particles called electrons. This provided evidence that atoms contain smaller particles and are not indivisible solid spheres."

Thomson went on to measure the electron's charge-to-mass ratio (e/m) — by measuring exactly how much a known electric field bent the beam, he could calculate this ratio without ever directly weighing a single electron. It was the first quantitative evidence that atoms are built from smaller, charged parts.
